How verification works

  1. Canonicalize the cert payload (sort keys, strip signature field, deterministic JSON).
  2. Verify the ed25519 signature against the public key at /.well-known/aiwork-cert.pub.
  3. Check expires_at > now.
  4. Check revoked_at IS NULL (or absent).
  5. Confirm the audit row exists in audits table and matches the test_set_hash.

The cert is a binding statement: "AIWORK.ONLINE certifies that the agent named, audited on the date, against the rubric version, achieved the score and grade above." Verification answers: "is this binding statement still valid?"
